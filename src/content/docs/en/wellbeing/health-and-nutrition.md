## Summary

YWAM Sendai values physical wellbeing as part of faithful and sustainable Christian life. Health is not treated as a private concern only. It affects energy, participation, relationships, and long-term fruitfulness in community and mission.

This page explains the general approach to health, nutrition, and personal responsibility at YWAM Sendai.

## Why does health matter in community life?

Health matters because physical wellbeing affects:
- daily participation
- emotional resilience
- learning and concentration
- ministry capacity
- rest and recovery
- the ability to care for others well

YWAM Sendai encourages community members to take reasonable responsibility for their health as part of wise stewardship.

## What is expected regarding personal health responsibility?

Community members are expected to take reasonable responsibility for their own health.

This includes:
- eating adequately and regularly
- sleeping enough
- staying hydrated
- managing medication responsibly
- seeking help when needed
- communicating important health concerns appropriately

YWAM Sendai is a community, but it is not a substitute for personal responsibility.

## What is the approach to nutrition?

YWAM Sendai encourages healthy and sustainable eating habits rather than extreme or careless patterns.

This includes:
- eating enough to support daily life and responsibilities
- making reasonable food choices
- respecting shared meal arrangements
- communicating significant dietary needs clearly
- avoiding patterns that seriously undermine health or community life

In shared community settings, healthy nutrition also includes practical stewardship such as regular meals, hydration, and not neglecting physical needs during busy seasons.

## What if someone has allergies, medical needs, or dietary restrictions?

Community members should communicate important medical, allergy, or dietary needs clearly and early.

This is especially important when the need may affect:
- shared meals
- housing
- travel
- school participation
- emergency response

Where possible, important dietary or allergy information should be communicated before arrival or during onboarding.

YWAM Sendai will seek to respond appropriately within reasonable limits, but clear communication is essential.

## What about exercise and physical care?

YWAM Sendai encourages ordinary physical stewardship. This may include:
- regular movement or exercise
- healthy sleep habits
- attention to stress and fatigue
- practical care for the body during busy seasons

The goal is not performance or image, but sustainable wellbeing.

## What if someone becomes sick?

If someone becomes sick, they should communicate appropriately with the relevant leader, staff member, or team contact.

Depending on the situation, this may involve:
- rest
- practical support
- medical consultation
- temporary adjustment of responsibilities
- follow-up communication

People should not ignore significant illness or assume they must push through everything alone.

## Does YWAM Sendai provide medical care?

YWAM Sendai is not a medical provider. Community members remain responsible for seeking appropriate medical care when needed.

YWAM Sendai may offer practical help, guidance, or support in navigating a situation, but it does not replace professional medical care.
