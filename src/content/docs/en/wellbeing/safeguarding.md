## Summary

YWAM Sendai is committed to safeguarding people from abuse, exploitation, harassment, coercion, and other forms of harmful conduct. Safeguarding is part of Christian responsibility, community trust, and wise leadership.

This page explains the general safeguarding commitment of YWAM Sendai and the kinds of concerns that should be taken seriously.

## What is safeguarding?

Safeguarding means protecting people, especially children and vulnerable adults, from abuse, neglect, exploitation, harassment, coercion, and unsafe treatment.

At YWAM Sendai, safeguarding also includes:
- creating a culture where concerns can be raised
- respecting appropriate boundaries
- handling power responsibly
- taking vulnerable people seriously
- not ignoring harmful or unsafe behavior

## Why does safeguarding matter?

Safeguarding matters because Christian community must not tolerate abuse, manipulation, or unsafe conduct.

It matters for:
- the protection of children and vulnerable adults
- the health of students, staff, visitors, and guests
- the integrity of leadership
- the credibility of ministry
- the witness of the Gospel

## What kinds of concerns fall under safeguarding?

Safeguarding concerns may include:
- abuse or suspected abuse
- sexual misconduct
- harassment
- coercion or manipulation
- inappropriate use of authority or power
- neglect of vulnerable people
- unsafe boundaries with children or vulnerable adults
- behavior that creates fear, intimidation, or exploitation

Safeguarding also includes maintaining appropriate boundaries in one-to-one situations, especially with children and vulnerable adults, and avoiding unnecessary private or isolated settings where concerns could arise.

## Who should take safeguarding concerns seriously?

Everyone in the community should take safeguarding concerns seriously.

This includes:
- staff
- students
- leaders
- volunteers
- visitors
- guests

No one should assume that a concern is too small to mention if it may involve harm, abuse, or unsafe conduct.

## What should someone do if they have a safeguarding concern?

A person with a safeguarding concern should report it promptly to the appropriate YWAM Sendai leader or safeguarding contact.

If someone is in immediate danger, immediate safety and emergency response come first.

People should not try to manage serious safeguarding concerns only through private informal conversation if the issue may involve abuse, coercion, or risk to others.

## How does YWAM Sendai respond to safeguarding concerns?

YWAM Sendai seeks to respond with seriousness, care, confidentiality, and appropriate action.

Depending on the situation, this may include:
- receiving and documenting the concern
- taking immediate protective steps
- limiting contact or access where necessary
- involving appropriate leadership
- seeking outside professional or legal guidance where required
- supporting affected individuals appropriately

## Does confidentiality matter?

Yes. Safeguarding concerns should be handled with appropriate confidentiality.

However, confidentiality does not mean secrecy that protects harmful behavior. Information may need to be shared with the appropriate people in order to protect others and respond responsibly.

## Is safeguarding only about children?

No. Safeguarding includes children, but it is not limited to them.

It also includes:
- vulnerable adults
- students in dependent situations
- people affected by power imbalance
- anyone at risk of abuse, coercion, or exploitation
