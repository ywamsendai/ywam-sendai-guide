## Summary

This page provides the bank account details used by YWAM Sendai for approved transfers and payments.

Please read the notes below carefully, since the correct account may depend on whether you are sending money from outside Japan or within Japan.

## Which account should I use?

A simple guide:

- **If you are sending money through Wise from outside Japan:** use the GMO Aozora Net Bank account only
- **If you are making a domestic transfer within Japan:** you may use either the GMO Aozora Net Bank account or the Japan Post Bank account

If you are unsure which account to use, contact:
- **finance@ywamsendai.org**

## GMO Aozora Net Bank

This account should be used for:
- Wise transfers from outside Japan
- domestic bank transfers within Japan, if preferred

### Account details

- **Bank Name:** GMO Aozora Net Bank
- **Bank Code:** 0310
- **Branch Name:** Houjin
- **Branch Code:** 101
- **Account Type:** Savings / Futsuu
- **Account Number:** 2042380
- **Account Name:** セカイセイネンセンキョウカイ

## Wise recipient details

If you are sending money through Wise, use the GMO account above.

Additional recipient details may include:

- **Recipient Type:** Business / Charity
- **Email:** finance@ywamsendai.org
- **Address:** 2-15-3 Kanmakicho, Takatsuki, Osaka, 569-0003

## Japan Post Bank (ゆうちょ銀行)

This account may be used for domestic transfers within Japan.

### Account details

- **Bank Name:** Japan Post Bank (ゆうちょ銀行)
- **Symbol (Kigo):** 10140
- **Number (Bango):** 65305041
- **Account Name:** セカイセイネンセンキョウカイ

## Important notes

- Wise transfers from outside Japan should go to the **GMO Aozora Net Bank** account only
- Domestic transfers within Japan may go to **either** approved bank account
- If your bank requires additional transfer information, contact **finance@ywamsendai.org**
