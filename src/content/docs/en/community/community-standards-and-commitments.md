## Summary

Community life at YWAM Sendai is shaped by shared spiritual, relational, and practical commitments. These standards are not intended to create unnecessary control or formality. They exist to help the community live faithfully, serve responsibly, and maintain healthy relationships with one another, with local churches, and with the wider people among whom YWAM Sendai ministers.

These commitments apply across the community, although the exact responsibilities may differ by role. Staff, students, teams, and visitors are all expected to respect the standards that shape life together at YWAM Sendai.

## Why does YWAM Sendai have shared standards and commitments?

YWAM Sendai has shared standards and commitments because long-term mission requires more than vision alone. It also requires shared convictions, healthy boundaries, and practical patterns of life that support faithfulness over time.

These commitments help the community:
- stay centered on Jesus Christ
- pursue integrity in both public ministry and private life
- cultivate healthy relationships and mutual trust
- support sustainable rhythms of work, rest, and worship
- serve in Japan with humility and credibility
- remain aligned with the historic Christian faith and with the ministry context in which YWAM Sendai serves

## Who do these standards apply to?

These standards apply across the YWAM Sendai community, including:
- long-term staff
- students
- outreach teams
- short-term teams
- interns where applicable
- visitors and guests where relevant

Not every person carries the same role or level of responsibility, but everyone is expected to respect the shared commitments of the community while participating in life at YWAM Sendai.

## What spiritual commitments shape community life?

YWAM Sendai expects community life to be rooted in devotion to Jesus Christ.

This includes shared commitment to:
- prayer
- worship
- Scripture
- repentance
- teachability
- obedience
- dependence on God rather than performance alone

The goal is not outward religious activity without inward reality. YWAM Sendai seeks a community life in which ministry flows from genuine life with God.

## What relational commitments shape community life?

YWAM Sendai expects people in the community to relate to one another with honesty, humility, and love.

This includes:
- speaking truthfully
- avoiding gossip and unnecessary division
- addressing conflict responsibly
- honoring appropriate boundaries
- showing respect across cultures, ages, and roles
- taking responsibility for the health of shared relationships
- practicing forgiveness, patience, and self-control

Community life cannot be healthy if people avoid responsibility for their words, attitudes, or relational conduct.

## What practical commitments shape community life?

YWAM Sendai expects people in the community to participate responsibly in the practical life of the mission.

This includes:
- respecting schedules and shared rhythms
- contributing to meals, duties, and common spaces where applicable
- honoring communication expectations
- caring for shared property and resources
- following safety, legal, and administrative requirements
- responding responsibly to leadership direction and community needs

Practical faithfulness is part of Christian maturity. Ordinary responsibilities matter.

## How does YWAM Sendai approach hospitality?

YWAM Sendai sees hospitality as an important part of Christian community life. Welcoming guests is not only a logistical task, but a way of expressing care, clarity, and respect.

This includes:
- preparing spaces with cleanliness and thoughtfulness
- helping guests understand the rhythms of the community
- greeting visitors warmly and respectfully
- including people without pressuring them
- respecting the need for rest, privacy, and cultural sensitivity

Hospitality at YWAM Sendai is meant to help people feel safe, welcomed, and honored.

## What standards of personal conduct does YWAM Sendai expect?

YWAM Sendai expects people in the community to live with integrity in both public and private life.

This includes integrity in areas such as:
- speech
- relationships
- sexuality
- use of time
- finances
- digital behavior
- treatment of others
- public representation of YWAM Sendai

People participating in the community should not treat private conduct as unrelated to ministry life, discipleship, or witness.

## What does YWAM Sendai teach about marriage, sex, and gender?

YWAM Sendai expects all staff, students, teams, and visitors to respect the ministry's Christian understanding of sex, marriage, and relationships.

In keeping with historic Christian belief and the theological convictions under which this ministry serves:
- marriage is understood as the covenant union of one man and one woman
- sexual relationships are expected to be kept within that covenant
- people serving within the community are expected to live in a way that aligns with their biological sex

These expectations are not included to target particular individuals, but to provide clarity about the theological and ministry framework of YWAM Sendai. They also reflect the importance of maintaining trust, unity, and credible witness in relationship with local churches and the wider ministry context in Japan.

## Does YWAM Sendai expect cultural humility?

Yes. YWAM Sendai serves in Japan and expects people in the community to approach this context with humility, respect, and a willingness to learn.

This includes:
- listening before assuming
- honoring local culture and relationships
- avoiding entitlement or superiority
- taking language and cultural learning seriously where applicable
- recognizing that ministry in Japan requires patience, humility, and credibility

People should not assume that sincerity alone is enough to serve well cross-culturally.

## How do rest, worship, and shared rhythms fit into community standards?

YWAM Sendai believes that healthy mission life requires shared rhythms, not only individual sincerity.

This includes commitment to rhythms such as:
- worship and prayer
- shared meals
- church participation
- work and service
- rest and Sabbath
- hospitality
- community communication

These rhythms help the community remain grounded, sustainable, and relationally healthy.

## Are these standards the same as a detailed rulebook?

No. These standards are not intended to function as an exhaustive rulebook for every possible situation.

They are shared commitments that help define the posture, boundaries, and expectations of life together at YWAM Sendai. More specific expectations may be explained in role-based pages, school guidance, onboarding materials, or safety policies.

## What happens if someone cannot agree with these commitments?

A person who cannot agree with the standards and commitments of YWAM Sendai should communicate honestly rather than pretending alignment.

In some cases, this may mean:
- further conversation for clarification
- adjustment of expectations
- reconsideration of participation
- a decision not to proceed with a role, school, team, or visit

Clarity is healthier than confusion, hidden disagreement, or avoidable strain within the community.
