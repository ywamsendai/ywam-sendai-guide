## Welcome

This handbook is a guide to the identity, mission, community life, training, and shared expectations of YWAM Sendai. It is designed to help applicants, students, staff, short-term visitors, supporters, and friends understand how YWAM Sendai lives and serves in Sendai and the wider Tohoku region of Japan.

Use the sections below to explore the handbook by topic or by role.
