## Summary

YWAM Sendai depends on God’s provision through the generosity of churches, individuals, families, and partners who share in the mission. Financial giving helps sustain the life, ministry, training, hospitality, and practical operations of the community.

This page explains the purpose of giving at YWAM Sendai and points to the appropriate finance pages for practical payment instructions.

## Why does giving matter?

Giving matters because YWAM Sendai is part of a missionary movement that depends on God’s provision through His people.

Financial giving helps support:
- ministry and outreach
- training and discipleship
- hospitality and community life
- practical operations
- long-term service in Sendai and Tohoku
- specific staff, projects, or needs where applicable

## Who can give?

Giving may come from:
- individuals
- families
- churches
- ministry partners
- supporters of specific staff or projects

## What kinds of giving may be possible?

Depending on how YWAM Sendai handles donations, giving may include:
- general donations
- support for specific staff
- support for specific projects
- one-time gifts
- recurring gifts

If you are unsure how your gift should be directed, it is best to contact YWAM Sendai before sending it.

## How should donations be made?

Donations should be made using the approved payment methods provided by YWAM Sendai.

Depending on your location and situation, this may include:
- Wise for international payments
- domestic bank transfer within Japan
- alternative methods such as card payment or PayPal where available

For exact instructions, see the finance pages linked below.

## Important note about payment methods

Payment methods and account details may vary depending on whether you are:
- giving from outside Japan
- making a domestic transfer within Japan
- using an alternative payment platform

For this reason, donors should always check the current finance instructions before sending money.

## Questions about giving

If you have questions about donations, designated giving, or the best payment route, please contact YWAM Sendai before sending your gift.
