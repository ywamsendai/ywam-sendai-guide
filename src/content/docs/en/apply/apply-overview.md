## Summary

YWAM Sendai uses one main application form for several types of applicants. This includes students, long-term staff, short-term teams, and individual visitors. The form is designed to route each applicant into the appropriate process.

Application to YWAM Sendai is not only administrative. It is also a process of discernment, preparation, and communication.

## Who can apply?

The main application form is used by:
- students applying for a YWAM school such as DTS or DBS
- people applying to join long-term staff
- short-term teams
- individual visitors or short-term participants

## Is there one application form?

Yes. YWAM Sendai uses a unified application form. Applicants select the category that best matches their situation, and the process continues from there.

Use the main application form here:

[Apply to YWAM Sendai](https://ywamsendai.org/en/apply)

## What are the general eligibility requirements?

General eligibility requirements include:
- minimum age of 18 years old, or 17 if the applicant will turn 18 during their time with YWAM Sendai
- functional proficiency in either English or Japanese
- any additional role-specific requirements listed in the relevant application pages

Some roles or programs have additional prerequisites. For example, long-term staff applicants must have completed a YWAM Discipleship Training School (DTS).

## What happens after I apply?

What happens next depends on the type of application.

In general, the process includes:
- review of the submitted application
- follow-up communication from YWAM Sendai
- any role-specific references, interviews, or discernment steps
- acceptance, decline, or invitation to continue the process

## What if I am not sure which category applies to me?

If you are unsure whether you should apply as a student, staff member, team, or visitor, contact YWAM Sendai before submitting the form. It is better to clarify your path early than to begin the wrong process.
