## Summary

YWAM Sendai welcomes contact from prospective students, prospective staff, outreach teams, guests, churches, supporters, and others who want to learn more about the community and its mission.

Because many of our audience are young people, messaging apps are often the easiest and most natural way to contact us. Email is still available, but it is not the main way most people first connect.

## Best ways to contact YWAM Sendai

For most people, the best way to begin is through one of our messaging platforms:

- **Discord:** an open community space and one of the main ways people connect with YWAM Sendai
- **LINE:** especially useful for Japanese-language inquiries
- **Instagram messages:** useful for informal questions and first contact

If you prefer email, you can also contact us at:
- **hello@ywamsendai.org**

## How is Discord used?

YWAM Sendai’s Discord server is open to external people and functions as an ongoing communication space for the wider community.

It includes:
- previous DTS graduates
- current participants
- people connected for Christian community
- communication for future DTS, DBS, and other schools

For many people, Discord is one of the easiest ways to begin connecting with YWAM Sendai and to stay in touch over time.

## When should I use the application form?

If you are interested in taking a more concrete next step, the application form is usually the best route.

This includes:
- student applications
- staff applications
- outreach team inquiries
- guest or visitor stay inquiries

If the form is the main intake route for these kinds of requests, it is often better than sending a long email first.

## Which contact method should I use?

A simple guide:

- **General questions or first contact:** Discord, LINE, or Instagram
- **Japanese-language inquiries:** LINE
- **If you prefer email:** hello@ywamsendai.org
- **Students, staff, outreach teams, guests, or visitors wanting to take a next step:** use the application form

## What should I include in my message?

To help YWAM Sendai respond clearly and efficiently, it is helpful to include:
- your name
- the reason for contacting
- any relevant dates or timing
- your main question
- any important background information

If you are interested in applying, visiting, joining a team, or staying as a guest, it is especially helpful to explain your situation clearly.

## What should I expect after contacting YWAM Sendai?

Response times may vary depending on:
- staff availability
- ministry season
- travel schedules
- holidays
- the nature of the inquiry

YWAM Sendai appreciates patience, especially during busy periods.
