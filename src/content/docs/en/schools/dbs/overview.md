## Summary

Discipleship Bible School (DBS) is a YWAM school focused on deep engagement with Scripture. At YWAM Sendai, DBS is designed to help students know God through His Word, strengthen their biblical foundation, and grow in faithful interpretation and application of Scripture.

DBS is not only about gaining information. It is about being formed by the Bible in mind, heart, and life.

DBS is a **12-week** school in which students read through the entire Bible chronologically and study Scripture as one unified story of God's redemptive purposes.

## What is DBS?

DBS stands for Discipleship Bible School.

It is a Bible-focused YWAM school that helps students engage deeply with Scripture through sustained reading, study, interpretation, and reflection.

At YWAM Sendai, DBS emphasizes:
- reading the whole Bible
- understanding Scripture in chronological flow
- learning historical and literary context
- recognizing the unity of the biblical story
- applying Scripture faithfully in life and ministry

## Who can apply for DBS?

DBS is intended for students who have already completed a YWAM Discipleship Training School (DTS).

At YWAM Sendai, completion of DTS is required before beginning DBS.

## What is the purpose of DBS?

The purpose of DBS is to help students:
- know God more deeply through Scripture
- understand the Bible as a unified revelation of God's character and purposes
- grow in biblical literacy
- develop habits of careful reading and interpretation
- apply Scripture faithfully in life and ministry

DBS also helps students grow in confidence and integrity in handling the Word of God.

## How is DBS studied at YWAM Sendai?

DBS at YWAM Sendai is built around a chronological and immersive approach to Scripture.

Students read through the Bible from Genesis to Revelation in the order of the biblical storyline, while learning to pay attention to:
- historical setting
- literary genre
- authorial intent
- major themes
- the unfolding story of redemption

The school also includes:
- classroom teaching
- small-group processing
- discussion and reflection
- written work and assignments
- community life and shared spiritual rhythms

## What is DBS like at YWAM Sendai?

DBS at YWAM Sendai is shaped by the wider mission and community life of the base. Students should expect Bible study within a context that also values:
- worship and prayer
- discipleship
- humility and obedience
- community participation
- mission awareness
- life in Sendai and the wider Tohoku context

## Who is DBS for?

DBS is for people who want to:
- deepen their understanding of Scripture
- strengthen their biblical foundation
- grow in disciplined engagement with the Bible
- prepare for ministry, leadership, or further training
- be formed by God's Word in a focused community setting

## Is DBS only for future teachers or leaders?

No. DBS is valuable for a wide range of students, not only those preparing for formal teaching roles.

It is appropriate for anyone who wants to become more grounded in Scripture and more faithful in understanding and applying the Bible.

## What should students expect from DBS?

Students should expect:
- serious engagement with Scripture
- regular reading and academic responsibility
- thoughtful participation in class and discussion
- community life and shared rhythms
- spiritual formation alongside study
- a learning environment that requires discipline, attentiveness, and perseverance

## Is there an outreach component?

DBS is primarily a lecture-phase school focused on Scripture. In some cases, students may have the opportunity to join a cross-cultural outreach after the lecture phase.

Where such an opportunity is offered, details will be communicated separately.
