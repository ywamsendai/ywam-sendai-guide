## Summary

This page lists optional resources that may help community members grow in language learning, cultural understanding, spiritual formation, and sustainable life in mission.

These resources are not required reading unless a leader or school specifically assigns them. They are provided as helpful recommendations for those who want to go deeper.

## What kinds of resources are included here?

The resources on this page are grouped into four broad categories:
- Japanese language tools
- Japan and cultural understanding
- YWAM and spiritual formation
- healthy life and sustainable mission

## Japanese language tools

These tools may be helpful for building vocabulary, reading ability, and everyday language confidence.

- **Anki**  
  A spaced-repetition flashcard tool that many learners use for vocabulary review.

- **WaniKani**  
  A structured tool for learning kanji and radicals.

- **Satori Reader**  
  A graded reading tool that can help bridge the gap between textbook study and real Japanese reading.

- **Dictionary apps**  
  A reliable Japanese dictionary app can be very useful for daily life, study, and ministry preparation.

## Japan and cultural understanding

These resources may help readers think more carefully about Japanese history, culture, communication, and religious context.

- **Silence** by Shusaku Endo  
  A well-known novel that raises important questions about faith, suffering, and Christian witness in Japan.

- **The Chrysanthemum and the Sword** by Ruth Benedict  
  A historically influential but dated work on Japanese culture. It may be useful as background reading, but it should not be treated as a complete or final guide to contemporary Japan.

- **Japan: The Paradox of Harmony** by Keiko Hirata  
  A resource that may help readers reflect on social dynamics, public life, and cultural tension in Japan.

Readers should approach cultural resources with humility and avoid treating any single book as a complete explanation of Japanese people or society.

## YWAM and spiritual formation

These resources may help community members understand YWAM’s history, Christian discipleship, and long-term spiritual formation.

- **Is That Really You, God?** by Loren Cunningham  
  A foundational YWAM book focused on hearing God’s voice and the early story of the mission.

- **The Spirit of the Disciplines** by Dallas Willard  
  A widely used book on spiritual formation and the practices that shape Christian life.

- **A Guide to Living in the Truth** by Michael Casey  
  A reflective work on humility, self-knowledge, and spiritual maturity.

## Healthy life and sustainable mission

These resources may be helpful for thinking about focus, rest, health, and sustainable patterns of life and work.

- **Deep Work** by Cal Newport  
  A book about concentration, attention, and meaningful work in a distracted environment.

- **Why We Sleep** by Matthew Walker  
  A book focused on the importance of sleep and recovery.

- **How Not to Die** by Michael Greger  
  A book focused on nutrition and long-term health from a whole-food perspective.

Community members should use discernment with health-related resources and should not treat general reading as a substitute for professional medical advice.

## Are these resources required?

No. These resources are optional unless they are specifically assigned in a school, training context, or leadership setting.
