## Summary

YWAM Sendai is a Christian missional community in Sendai, Japan. It is part of Youth With A Mission (YWAM), a global movement that equips people to know God and make Him known through discipleship, training, evangelism, mercy ministry, and service.

YWAM Sendai exists to serve Sendai and the wider Tohoku region through Christian community, ministry, training, hospitality, and long-term presence. Its work includes schools, local outreach, prayer, relationship-building, and partnership with the wider body of Christ in Japan.

## What is YWAM?

Youth With A Mission (YWAM) is an international Christian movement. YWAM brings together people from many nations, cultures, and church backgrounds who share a desire to follow Jesus and serve others.

YWAM ministries around the world commonly focus on:
- discipleship and spiritual formation
- Bible training and leadership development
- evangelism and outreach
- mercy ministry and practical service
- worship, prayer, and intercession
- partnership with local churches and communities

## What is YWAM Sendai?

YWAM Sendai is the YWAM expression based in Sendai, the largest city in the Tohoku region of northeastern Japan.

YWAM Sendai is not only a training location or event host. It is a long-term missional community that seeks to live faithfully in one place over time. Its life includes:
- shared prayer and worship
- discipleship and spiritual formation
- local ministry in Sendai
- hospitality and community life
- training through YWAM schools
- service to the church and wider society in Tohoku

## What does YWAM Sendai do?

YWAM Sendai serves through a combination of community life, ministry, and training. This includes:
- running YWAM schools such as DTS and DBS
- equipping students and staff for Christian service
- building relationships in the local community
- participating in local church life
- serving people in practical and relational ways
- cultivating prayerful, long-term presence in Tohoku

The exact shape of ministry may change over time, but the core commitment remains the same: to love God, love people, and serve the region faithfully.

## Where is YWAM Sendai located?

YWAM Sendai is located in Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, Japan. Sendai is the major urban center of the Tohoku region and serves as a strategic base for ministry, training, and regional relationships.

## Who is YWAM Sendai for?

YWAM Sendai serves several groups of people, including:
- people exploring Christian discipleship and mission
- students applying for YWAM schools
- long-term staff and missionaries
- short-term teams and visitors
- local friends, churches, and ministry partners
- supporters and donors who want to invest in the work in Tohoku

## How is YWAM Sendai connected to the wider YWAM movement?

YWAM Sendai is part of the wider YWAM family and shares the foundational beliefs, values, and mission of YWAM internationally. At the same time, YWAM Sendai has a specific local calling shaped by the spiritual, cultural, and social realities of Sendai and Tohoku.

For more about YWAM-wide beliefs and values, see the YWAM reference pages in this handbook.

## What makes YWAM Sendai distinct?

YWAM Sendai is shaped by its long-term commitment to the Tohoku region. Its identity emphasizes:
- faithful presence rather than busyness
- community life rooted in prayer and worship
- cultural humility and language learning
- partnership with the Japanese church
- discipleship that forms both character and calling
- a desire to serve the renewal of Tohoku over the long term
