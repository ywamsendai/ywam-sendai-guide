## Summary

This page explains the general refund approach for students attending YWAM Sendai schools. Refund outcomes may depend on the stage of the school, the type of cost involved, and whether expenses have already been committed.

If a student needs to withdraw, cancel, or leave early, they should communicate with YWAM Sendai as soon as possible.

## General principle

In general, some school-related payments may be refundable in full or in part, while others may not be refundable once they have been paid or committed.

Refunds depend on:
- the type of payment
- the stage of the school
- whether costs have already been paid on the student's behalf
- whether any third-party fees or non-refundable expenses apply

## DTS Lecture Phase and DBS

For the DTS lecture phase and for DBS:

- tuition fees are generally non-refundable once paid
- exceptions may be discussed depending on the circumstances
- accommodation may be refunded on a pro-rated weekly basis where applicable

## Outreach Phase

Refunds during the outreach phase depend on timing and on which expenses have already been committed.

### If a student withdraws before outreach begins

The outreach amount may be refunded, less:
- any airline cancellation fee
- any outreach expenses already paid or committed

### If a student withdraws during outreach

A refund may be calculated based on the remaining period, less any non-refundable or already committed costs.

This may include:
- pro-rated accommodation by week
- pro-rated meals by week
- airline adjustment costs
- ticket change fees
- other non-refundable expenses already incurred

## Why refunds may be limited

Some school and outreach costs are paid in advance or become non-refundable once bookings, travel, accommodation, or other arrangements have been made.

For this reason, a refund may be partial rather than full, even if a student does not complete the full program.

## What should a student do if they need to withdraw or cancel?

If a student needs to withdraw, cancel, or discuss a possible refund, they should contact YWAM Sendai as early as possible.

Early communication is important because it may affect:
- airline costs
- accommodation commitments
- outreach planning
- the amount that may still be refundable

## Important note

Refund decisions may require case-by-case review depending on the circumstances and the costs already incurred.
