## Summary

Applying to a YWAM Sendai school is a process of application, review, communication, and preparation. This page explains the normal admissions pathway for students applying to programs such as DTS or DBS.

Exact requirements and timelines may vary by school, intake, and applicant situation. This page gives the general structure of the student admissions process.

## Who is this page for?

This page is for people applying as students to a YWAM Sendai school.

If you are applying as long-term staff, a short-term team, or a visitor, use the role-specific pages for those categories instead.

## What is the student admissions process?

The normal student admissions process includes:
1. choosing the school or intake
2. submitting the application form
3. application review
4. references or follow-up communication where needed
5. admissions decision
6. deposit or confirmation steps where applicable
7. pre-arrival preparation

## Step 1: Choose the school or intake

Before applying, applicants should confirm which school or intake they are applying for.

This may include:
- Discipleship Training School (DTS)
- Discipleship Bible School (DBS)
- another approved training program offered by YWAM Sendai

Applicants should make sure they understand the purpose, timing, and basic requirements of the school before applying.

## Step 2: Submit the application form

Applicants begin by submitting the main YWAM Sendai application form and selecting the appropriate student category.

The application form gathers information such as:
- personal background
- church and spiritual background
- reasons for applying
- practical information relevant to study and community life

Use the main application form here:

[Apply to YWAM Sendai](../../../apply/apply-overview)

## Step 3: Application review

After the application is submitted, YWAM Sendai reviews the application to assess readiness and fit for the school.

This review may consider:
- general eligibility
- clarity of motivation
- readiness for community life
- suitability for the specific school
- practical timing and communication

## Step 4: References or follow-up communication

Some applications may require references or additional follow-up communication before a final decision is made.

This stage helps clarify:
- character and maturity
- church connection
- readiness for the school environment
- any practical concerns that need to be addressed before acceptance

## Step 5: Admissions decision

After review, YWAM Sendai will communicate the admissions decision.

The outcome may be:
- acceptance
- decline
- request for more information
- invitation to continue with additional steps

Acceptance means the applicant may move forward into confirmation and pre-arrival preparation.

## Step 6: Deposit or confirmation steps

Accepted students may need to complete confirmation steps such as:
- confirming their place in the school
- paying any required deposit
- acknowledging key deadlines and expectations

The exact requirements depend on the school and intake.

## Step 7: Pre-arrival preparation

After acceptance, students move into pre-arrival preparation. This includes practical planning for travel, finances, documents, packing, communication, and arrival in Japan.

For more detail, see:
- [Student Pre-Arrival Preparation](../pre-arrival-preparation)

## Does meeting the requirements guarantee acceptance?

No. Meeting the basic requirements allows an application to be considered, but it does not guarantee acceptance.

YWAM Sendai also considers:
- readiness for the school
- suitability for community life
- available space
- timing and practical feasibility
- overall fit with the purpose of the program

## How early should I apply?

Applicants should apply as early as possible. Early application gives more time for:
- review and communication
- references
- financial planning
- travel preparation
- visa-related planning where needed
