## Summary

Long-term staff joining YWAM Sendai need to prepare carefully for visa and immigration requirements before arriving in Japan. Visa processes vary depending on nationality, legal status, timing, and the nature of the person's stay.

This page explains the general role of visa and immigration preparation in the staff onboarding process. It does not replace official government guidance or case-specific instructions.

## Why does visa preparation matter?

Visa preparation matters because long-term staff should not assume that they can simply arrive in Japan and sort out legal status later.

Immigration requirements affect:
- whether a person can enter Japan
- how long they can stay
- what documentation is needed
- when travel can realistically happen
- what preparation must be completed before arrival

## Does every staff member follow the same visa process?

No. Visa and immigration requirements vary depending on factors such as:
- nationality
- current country of residence
- previous immigration history
- intended length of stay
- current Japanese legal status, if any

Because of this, staff should not rely on another person's experience as if it automatically applies to them.

## Who is responsible for checking visa requirements?

The staff applicant is responsible for understanding and complying with the immigration requirements that apply to their own situation.

YWAM Sendai may help with guidance, documents, or coordination where appropriate, but it does not replace official immigration authorities or personal legal responsibility.

## When should visa planning begin?

Visa planning should begin as early as possible during the onboarding process.

It should not be left until the final stage, because timing may be affected by:
- document preparation
- financial readiness
- application processing times
- travel planning
- changing immigration rules

## Does financial readiness affect visa preparation?

Yes. In normal circumstances, YWAM Sendai expects staff to reach the required level of financial readiness before visa-related processes move forward.

Staff should not plan for relocation to Japan without the practical means to live responsibly and meet their obligations after arrival.

## What kinds of preparation may be needed?

Depending on the situation, visa preparation may include:
- confirming what type of entry or status is appropriate
- gathering required documents
- coordinating with YWAM Sendai for supporting paperwork
- checking passport validity
- planning around processing timelines
- preparing for arrival procedures

## Can YWAM Sendai guarantee visa approval?

No. YWAM Sendai cannot guarantee visa approval or immigration outcomes.

Final decisions belong to the relevant government authorities. Staff should avoid making irreversible travel or relocation assumptions until the necessary permissions are in place.

## What should staff do if their situation changes?

If a staff applicant's immigration situation changes, they should inform YWAM Sendai promptly.

Examples include:
- passport renewal issues
- delays in documentation
- changes in legal status
- travel complications
- unexpected immigration questions or restrictions

Early communication helps avoid confusion and unrealistic planning.

## Where should staff get official information?

Staff should use official government sources and any case-specific instructions provided during onboarding.

YWAM Sendai guidance should be treated as supportive and practical, but not as a substitute for official immigration information.
