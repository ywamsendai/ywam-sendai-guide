## Summary

Joining long-term staff at YWAM Sendai is a commitment to shared mission, community life, and long-term service in Japan. Before beginning the staff application process, applicants must meet the basic prerequisites for staff service.

This page explains the main eligibility requirements for long-term staff applicants. It does not describe the full application process.

## Who is this page for?

This page is for people who want to apply to join YWAM Sendai as long-term staff.

If you are applying as a student, short-term team member, or visitor, use the role-specific pages for those categories instead.

## What are the basic prerequisites for staff?

Long-term staff applicants must:
- meet the general eligibility requirements for YWAM Sendai
- have successfully completed a YWAM Discipleship Training School (DTS) at a registered University of the Nations location
- be willing to join a pioneering, community-based ministry environment
- be prepared to pursue partnership development and self-support
- be willing to grow in Japanese language and cultural learning
- be open to discernment, references, and interview processes

## Is DTS required to apply for staff?

Yes. Completion of a YWAM Discipleship Training School (DTS) is a prerequisite for applying to join long-term staff at YWAM Sendai.

Applicants must have completed DTS through a registered University of the Nations location before beginning staff service.

## What kind of person is YWAM Sendai looking for in staff?

YWAM Sendai is looking for people who are ready to serve in a pioneering and community-based environment. This includes people who are:
- spiritually grounded and teachable
- flexible and adaptable
- willing to live in shared community
- committed to long-term growth rather than short-term excitement
- open to practical service as well as spiritual ministry
- willing to learn language and culture seriously
- able to contribute to the health and mission of the community

## Do staff need to raise their own support?

Yes. Long-term staff at YWAM Sendai are self-supported.

This means accepted staff members are expected to develop financial support and reach the required support level before joining the team in Sendai.

More detailed finance guidance is provided in the staff finance pages.

## Do staff need Japanese language ability before applying?

Applicants do not need to be fully fluent in Japanese before applying. However, they must be willing to become active learners of the Japanese language.

Long-term staff are expected to pursue language growth seriously as part of faithful service in Japan.

## Does meeting the prerequisites guarantee acceptance?

No. Meeting the prerequisites allows an applicant to enter the staff discernment and application process, but it does not guarantee acceptance.

YWAM Sendai also considers:
- character
- readiness for community life
- suitability for a pioneering environment
- references and interview outcomes
- alignment with the mission and culture of YWAM Sendai

## What happens after I meet the prerequisites?

If you meet the prerequisites and want to continue, the next step is the staff application and onboarding process.
