## Summary

Joining long-term staff at YWAM Sendai is a process of discernment, preparation, and practical planning. The process includes application, references, interviews, support raising, and pre-arrival onboarding.

This page explains the normal pathway from initial application to arrival in Sendai. Exact timing may vary depending on the applicant's situation, nationality, support progress, and visa requirements.

## Who is this process for?

This process is for people who want to join YWAM Sendai as long-term staff.

Before beginning this process, applicants should first review:
- [Staff Eligibility and Prerequisites](../eligibility-and-prerequisites)

## What is the staff application process?

The normal staff application and onboarding process includes the following stages:
1. initial application
2. references and review
3. interview and discernment
4. acceptance
5. support raising and preparation
6. visa and arrival planning
7. onboarding after arrival

## Step 1: Initial application

Applicants begin by submitting the main YWAM Sendai application form and selecting the appropriate staff category.

The application form is used to gather:
- personal background information
- ministry and church background
- YWAM training history
- reasons for applying
- practical information relevant to staff service

Use the main application form here:

[Apply to YWAM Sendai](../../../apply/apply-overview)

## Step 2: References and review

After the application is submitted, YWAM Sendai reviews the application and follows up with the applicant's references as needed.

This stage helps YWAM Sendai understand:
- the applicant's character and maturity
- ministry background
- readiness for community life
- suitability for long-term service in Japan

## Step 3: Interview and discernment

If the application moves forward, YWAM Sendai will normally schedule one or more interviews or discernment conversations.

These conversations help both the applicant and YWAM Sendai consider:
- calling and motivation
- readiness for pioneering ministry
- expectations for community life
- language and cultural learning
- financial support and timing
- long-term fit with the mission of YWAM Sendai

## Step 4: Acceptance

If the discernment process is positive, YWAM Sendai may offer acceptance to join long-term staff.

Acceptance means the applicant may continue into the preparation stage. It does not mean that every practical requirement has already been completed.

## Step 5: Support raising and preparation

Accepted staff members are expected to develop financial support before joining the team in Sendai.

This stage usually includes:
- clarifying the required support level
- building a support team
- communicating with sending churches, friends, and partners
- preparing financially for relocation and long-term service

Staff should not assume they can arrive immediately after acceptance. In most cases, support development is a necessary part of preparation.

## Step 6: Visa and arrival planning

Once support, timing, and eligibility are in place, YWAM Sendai works with the accepted staff member on visa and arrival planning where applicable.

This may include:
- confirming nationality-specific visa requirements
- preparing required documents
- coordinating timing for arrival
- discussing housing and practical setup
- planning travel to Japan

For more detail, see:
- [Staff Visa and Immigration](../visa-and-immigration)

## Step 7: Onboarding after arrival

After arrival in Sendai, new staff enter an onboarding period that helps them settle into the life and mission of YWAM Sendai.

This may include:
- orientation to community life
- introduction to rhythms, responsibilities, and expectations
- practical setup for housing and daily life
- relationship-building with the team
- initial ministry integration
- continued language and cultural learning

## How long does the process take?

The timeline varies. It depends on factors such as:
- how quickly the application and references are completed
- interview scheduling
- support raising progress
- visa requirements
- housing and arrival timing

Because of these variables, applicants should begin the process early and communicate clearly with YWAM Sendai.

## What should applicants prepare during this process?

Applicants should be prepared to work on:
- prayerful discernment
- honest communication
- support development
- practical planning for relocation
- language learning readiness
- flexibility regarding timing
