## Summary

Japanese language development is an important part of long-term staff life at YWAM Sendai. Staff are not expected to begin with full fluency, but they are expected to become serious and active learners of Japanese over time.

Language development is not treated as an optional side project. It is part of faithful presence, healthy team life, and long-term ministry in Japan.

## Why does language development matter?

Language development matters because YWAM Sendai serves in Japan, not in an isolated international bubble. Learning Japanese helps staff:
- build real relationships
- participate more fully in local life
- serve with humility and respect
- understand culture more deeply
- reduce dependence on translation
- contribute to long-term ministry fruitfulness in Tohoku

Language learning is part of loving people well in their own context.

## Is Japanese fluency required before joining staff?

No. Applicants do not need to be fully fluent in Japanese before joining long-term staff.

However, applicants must be willing to:
- begin learning seriously
- continue learning consistently
- accept that language growth is part of their staff responsibility

A resistant or passive attitude toward language learning is not compatible with long-term staff service at YWAM Sendai.

## What level of Japanese is expected over time?

YWAM Sendai expects long-term staff to pursue meaningful progress in Japanese over time.

The exact pace may vary depending on:
- previous language background
- role and responsibilities
- time in Japan
- learning opportunities and season of life

Where applicable, YWAM Sendai may use goals such as JLPT-related benchmarks to help clarify progress expectations.

## What kind of attitude is expected toward language learning?

YWAM Sendai expects staff to approach language learning with:
- humility
- consistency
- patience
- teachability
- perseverance

Language development can be slow and demanding. Staff are expected to keep learning even when progress feels gradual.

## Is language learning only about vocabulary and grammar?

No. Language development also includes cultural learning, listening, and relational sensitivity.

At YWAM Sendai, language development includes:
- practical Japanese communication
- cultural observation and understanding
- learning how to listen well
- growing in respect for local ways of communicating
- becoming more at home in the context of Japan

## How is language development supported?

Language development may be supported through a combination of:
- personal study
- formal classes where available
- team expectations and accountability
- immersion in daily life
- ministry participation
- bilingual community interaction

The exact structure may vary by season and by the staff member's role.

## Does every staff role require the same level of Japanese?

Not necessarily. Different roles may involve different communication demands.

However, all long-term staff are expected to move toward deeper language ability over time. No staff role should be treated as permanently exempt from language growth.

## How does language development relate to mission?

Language development is part of mission because it helps staff become more rooted, more relational, and more useful in the local context.

It reflects a commitment to:
- incarnational ministry
- cultural humility
- long-term presence
- service that is understandable and respectful
