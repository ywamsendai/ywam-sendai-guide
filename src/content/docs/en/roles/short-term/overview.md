## Summary

YWAM Sendai welcomes some short-term visitors and teams for limited periods of participation, service, learning, or relationship-building. Short-term involvement can be valuable, but it works best when it is approached with humility, flexibility, and respect for the local context.

This page gives a general overview of short-term visits and teams at YWAM Sendai.

## Who is this page for?

This page is for:
- short-term teams
- individual short-term visitors
- people exploring a temporary stay with YWAM Sendai
- churches or groups considering a visit

## What counts as a short-term visit?

A short-term visit is a temporary period of participation with YWAM Sendai that does not fall under long-term staff service or full student enrollment in a school.

This may include:
- a short ministry visit
- a team visit
- a learning or exposure trip
- a temporary stay connected to relationship-building or service
- another approved short-term arrangement

## Are all short-term visits the same?

No. Short-term visits can vary significantly in purpose, length, structure, and level of participation.

Some visits may focus on:
- serving alongside ongoing ministry
- learning about the local context
- prayer and encouragement
- relationship-building
- practical help
- exploring possible future involvement

## How does YWAM Sendai view short-term teams?

YWAM Sendai values short-term teams when they come with the right posture and expectations.

A healthy short-term visit should support, not disrupt, the long-term life and mission of the community. This means short-term participation should be shaped by:
- humility
- listening
- flexibility
- respect for local leadership
- willingness to serve in ordinary ways
- realistic expectations about what a short visit can accomplish

## How do people apply for a short-term visit?

Short-term visitors and teams use the main YWAM Sendai application process unless instructed otherwise.

For more detail, see:
- [Apply to YWAM Sendai](../../../apply/apply-overview)

YWAM Sendai may also require additional communication to clarify the purpose, timing, and suitability of the visit.

For teams, this may include a follow-up conversation with the team leader about:
- dates
- team size
- ministry focus
- housing
- expectations for the visit

## Are all short-term visits accepted?

No. YWAM Sendai considers short-term requests carefully based on factors such as:
- timing
- team size
- housing capacity
- ministry suitability
- leadership capacity
- the purpose and posture of the visit

Not every short-term request will be the right fit for the season or needs of the community.

## What should short-term visitors expect?

Short-term visitors should expect:
- a community-based environment
- practical participation rather than a customized experience
- shared rhythms and expectations
- flexibility regarding schedule and role
- guidance from YWAM Sendai leadership
- a posture of learning as well as serving

## Does a short-term visit guarantee a specific ministry opportunity?

No. Short-term visitors should not assume that a visit guarantees a specific ministry platform, public role, or customized outreach opportunity.

The shape of the visit depends on:
- timing
- local needs
- team size
- housing and logistics
- leadership discernment
- the visitor's purpose and readiness

## Does YWAM Sendai sponsor short-term visas?

No. YWAM Sendai does not provide visa sponsorship for short-term visits.

Visitors are responsible for checking the entry requirements that apply to their nationality and situation and for ensuring that their passport and travel documents are valid.
