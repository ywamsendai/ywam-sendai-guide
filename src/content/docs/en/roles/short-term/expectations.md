## Summary

Short-term visitors and teams are expected to participate in ways that support the health, mission, and rhythms of YWAM Sendai. Because short-term guests enter an already existing community, they are expected to adapt respectfully rather than assume the community will adapt entirely around them.

This page explains the general expectations for short-term participation.

Short-term expectations should be read together with the wider [Community Standards and Commitments](../../../community/community-standards-and-commitments), which apply across staff, students, teams, and visitors. This page focuses on how those shared commitments apply in short-term participation.

## Who is this page for?

This page is for:
- individual short-term visitors
- short-term teams
- group leaders preparing a visit to YWAM Sendai

## What is expected of short-term visitors?

Short-term visitors are expected to:
- respect local leadership and guidance
- participate with humility and flexibility
- honor community rhythms and shared spaces
- communicate clearly and responsibly
- serve in practical ways as needed
- represent themselves and their sending context honestly
- avoid creating unnecessary burden for the host community

## What posture should short-term teams bring?

Short-term teams should come with a posture of:
- listening before assuming
- learning before evaluating
- serving before seeking visibility
- partnership rather than control
- encouragement rather than pressure

A short-term visit should not be driven by the need to produce a dramatic experience or visible results.

## Are short-term visitors expected to join community rhythms?

Yes. Visitors and teams are normally expected to participate in the rhythms that are appropriate to their stay.

This may include:
- meals
- worship or prayer times
- practical duties
- team meetings
- church participation
- ministry activities arranged by YWAM Sendai

The exact level of participation depends on the nature and length of the visit.

## What is expected in housing and shared spaces?

Short-term visitors are expected to care for shared spaces with maturity and respect.

This includes:
- cleanliness
- punctuality
- consideration for noise and rest
- cooperation with house expectations
- respect for property and facilities

Space in Japan is often limited, and visitors should be prepared for close shared living conditions.

## Are there cultural expectations visitors should take seriously?

Yes. Short-term visitors are expected to approach Japan with humility, attentiveness, and respect.

This includes:
- removing outdoor shoes when required in homes or shared indoor spaces
- keeping voice levels low in shared housing, on public transport, and in public places
- respecting local norms around personal space and physical contact
- observing carefully before assuming what is appropriate
- receiving hospitality graciously where possible

Visitors should come as learners, not as experts on the local context.

## Are there expectations around church and ministry settings?

Yes. Short-term visitors are expected to conduct themselves with maturity and cultural sensitivity in church and ministry settings.

This includes:
- dressing appropriately for church and ministry contexts
- remaining attentive and respectful during services and gatherings
- following local guidance about behavior, schedule, and participation
- understanding that public witness includes ordinary conduct, not only formal ministry moments

## What is expected in communication?

Short-term visitors and team leaders should communicate clearly before and during the visit.

This includes:
- being honest about goals and expectations
- informing YWAM Sendai about changes or problems
- responding to instructions in a timely way
- avoiding assumptions about schedule, ministry role, or logistics

## Are there behavioral expectations?

Yes. Short-term visitors are expected to conduct themselves in ways that reflect Christian integrity and support the wellbeing of the community.

This includes avoiding:
- disrespectful behavior
- dishonesty
- harmful relational conduct
- disregard for boundaries
- substance misuse
- behavior that creates confusion, pressure, or unnecessary disruption

## What responsibilities do team leaders carry?

Team leaders remain responsible for the care, conduct, and communication of their team while serving under the local leadership of YWAM Sendai.

This normally includes:
- helping the team follow local expectations
- addressing tensions or problems early
- maintaining clear communication with YWAM Sendai
- supporting the spiritual and relational health of team members
- helping the team act with maturity and unity

## What happens if expectations are not met?

If a short-term visitor or team does not meet expectations, YWAM Sendai may respond with:
- clarification
- conversation
- adjustment of participation
- limitation of activities
- ending the visit early in serious cases

The goal is to protect the health of the community and respond wisely to the situation.
