## Summary

Short-term visits work best when visitors arrive prepared. Preparation includes practical planning, clear communication, financial readiness, and the right posture for entering a local community and ministry environment.

This page explains the general preparation expected before a short-term visit to YWAM Sendai.

## Who is this page for?

This page is for:
- individual short-term visitors
- short-term teams
- team leaders coordinating a visit

## What should be prepared before arrival?

Short-term visitors should prepare:
- travel documents
- finances for the trip
- communication with YWAM Sendai
- realistic expectations for the visit
- appropriate clothing and personal items
- readiness for shared community life
- awareness of the local context in Japan

## What travel preparation is needed?

Visitors should make sure they understand and prepare:
- passport validity
- visa or entry requirements where applicable
- flight and arrival details
- local transportation planning if needed
- any arrival information requested by YWAM Sendai

Visitors are responsible for checking the travel requirements that apply to their nationality and situation.

## What financial preparation is needed?

Visitors should understand:
- what costs they are responsible for
- what payments are due before arrival
- what payment methods are available
- whether any costs must be paid in Japanese yen cash
- what personal spending money they should bring

Visitors should not assume that all costs are covered unless this has been clearly confirmed.

Team leaders should also make sure that team-related costs are handled responsibly and that any local bills are settled before departure.

## What communication should happen before arrival?

Before arrival, visitors or team leaders should communicate clearly with YWAM Sendai about:
- dates of the visit
- number of people coming
- purpose of the visit
- arrival details
- health or dietary needs that need to be known
- any major changes affecting the trip

Clear communication helps prevent confusion and unnecessary strain on the host community.

## What should team leaders prepare in advance?

Team leaders should prepare:
- clear communication with YWAM Sendai before arrival
- emergency contact information for team members
- passport information for the team where requested
- realistic expectations for schedule and ministry participation
- internal team communication and pastoral oversight

Team leaders should be ready to help their team adapt well to the local context rather than assuming the visit will run according to their own preferred structure.

## What kind of mindset should visitors prepare?

Short-term visitors should prepare to come with:
- humility
- flexibility
- willingness to learn
- respect for local leadership
- patience with uncertainty
- readiness to serve in ordinary ways

A short-term visit is not only about what visitors want to do. It is also about how they enter and support an existing community faithfully.

## What should visitors pack?

Visitors should pack according to:
- the season in Japan
- the length of the visit
- community living needs
- ministry and church settings
- personal daily needs

This normally includes:
- modest and appropriate clothing for church and ministry settings
- comfortable clothing for practical service and shared living
- personal toiletries and daily essentials
- any medication or health-related items needed
- a reusable water bottle where helpful

Visitors should avoid overpacking, but they should come prepared for practical daily life and shared living.

## What if a team has special plans or goals?

If a team has specific hopes, ministry ideas, or goals for the visit, these should be discussed with YWAM Sendai in advance.

Teams should not assume that all plans can be accommodated exactly as imagined. Final decisions about local participation and schedule belong to YWAM Sendai as host.
